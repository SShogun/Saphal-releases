Here is how to install Saphal on your Windows computer. It takes about 5 minutes.

1. Click this link to download:
https://github.com/SShogun/saphal-releases/releases/latest/download/Saphal-Setup.exe

2. Open the downloaded file "Saphal-Setup.exe" (it is in your Downloads folder).

3. Windows may show a blue box saying "Windows protected your PC". This is normal. It shows up because Saphal is a new app that Windows does not know yet.
   - Click "More info" (a small link in the box).
   - Then click "Run anyway".

4. Wait a few seconds. Saphal installs and opens. A Saphal icon appears on your desktop.

5. Sign in with the email and password your admin gave you. Saphal signs you out after 12 hours, so just sign in again.

If you have no email and password, or you forgot your password, ask your admin.

If Saphal says it cannot reach the server, check your internet, close and reopen Saphal, and wait a minute. If it still fails, contact your admin.

Thanks!
