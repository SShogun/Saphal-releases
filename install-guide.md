# Install Saphal

Saphal is a Windows app for tracking monthly work. This page shows you how to install it. It takes about 5 minutes.

**You need:**
- A Windows computer
- An internet connection
- The email and password your admin gave you

---

## Step 1: Download

Click this link:

https://github.com/SShogun/saphal-releases/releases/latest/download/Saphal-Setup.exe

A file called **Saphal-Setup.exe** will download. Look for it in your **Downloads** folder or at the bottom or top of your browser.

<!-- screenshot: browser showing the finished download -->

---

## Step 2: Open the file

Double-click **Saphal-Setup.exe**.

Windows will probably show a blue box that says **"Windows protected your PC"**. This is normal. Go to Step 3.

If you do not see the blue box, skip to Step 4.

---

## Step 3: The blue warning box

Windows shows this box because Saphal is a new app. Not many people have downloaded it yet, so Windows does not know it. It does not mean something is wrong with the file.

1. Click **More info**. This is a small link in the box. It is not a button.
2. A new button appears. Click **Run anyway**.

<!-- screenshot: blue box before clicking More info -->
<!-- screenshot: blue box after clicking More info, with the Run anyway button -->

If you do not see **Run anyway**, you did not click **More info** first.

---

## Step 4: Install

The installer opens by itself. Wait a few seconds. When it finishes, Saphal opens and a **Saphal** icon appears on your desktop.

You can use that icon to open Saphal next time.

<!-- screenshot: Saphal icon on the desktop -->

---

## Step 5: Sign in

Type the email and password your admin gave you. Then click **Sign in**.

<!-- screenshot: Saphal sign-in screen -->

Good to know:
- Saphal signs you out after **12 hours**. Sign in again with the same email and password.
- You cannot create your own account. Your admin makes it for you.
- If you forget your password, ask your admin to reset it. There is no "forgot password" email.

---

## If something goes wrong

**I do not have an email and password.**
Ask your admin.

**The sign-in says the email or password is wrong.**
Check for typos and capital letters. If it still fails, ask your admin to reset your password.

**Saphal says it cannot reach the server.**
1. Check that your internet works. Try opening any website.
2. Close Saphal and open it again.
3. Wait 1 minute and try again.
4. If it still fails, contact [CONTACT NAME, PHONE OR EMAIL] and tell them what the screen says.

**Saphal does not open.**
1. Double-click the **Saphal** icon on your desktop again.
2. If nothing happens, restart your computer and try again.
3. If it still does not open, run Step 1 to Step 4 again. This installs the newest version and keeps your account.

**My work computer will not let me install.**
Some work computers block new apps. Ask your IT person, or contact [CONTACT NAME, PHONE OR EMAIL].
